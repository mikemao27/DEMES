<h1 align = "center">Reading List: Toward Syntax, Semantics, and Meaning in AI</h1>

Here, we provide a working reading list on building artificial intelligence that develops certain syntax, semantics, and meaning encoding capabilities in artificial intelligence models. Most of these works could be claimed to be auto-regressive in nature. But, they are nonetheless impactful to the overall progression of language encoding and natural language processing capabilities in artificial intelligence.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These papers are all available in public domain. However, note that perhaps not all of these papers (but a majority of which) are provided here in PDF form. We do not claim to own any of the ideas or intellectual property behind these papers, but instead merely provide them as resources to gain an insight into the ideas that shaped the models we produce. To assist with paper retrieval, we provide links to each paper.

## Papers
* [Google - "Attention is All You Need" (2023)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762)
* [Develin et al. - "BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding" (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.04805)
* [Mikolov et al. - "Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space" (2013)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.3781)
* [Pennington et al. - "GloVe: Global Vectors for Word Representation" (2014)](https://aclanthology.org/D14-1162.pdf)
* [Socher et al. - "Recursive Deep Models for Semantic Compositionality Over a Sentiment Treebank" (2013)](https://aclanthology.org/D13-1170.pdf)
* [Peters et al. - "Deep Contextualized Word Representations" (2018)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.05365)
* [Conneau et al. - "Unsupervised Cross-lingual Representation Learning at Scale" (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.02116)
* [Piatadosi & Hill - "Meaning without Reference in Large Language Models" (2022)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=nRkJEwmZnM)
* [Bender & Koller - "Climbing towards NLU: On Meaning, Form, and Understanding in the Age of Data" (2020)](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.463.pdf)
* [Harnad - "The Symbol Grounding Problem" (1990)](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/activities/ieg/e-library/sources/harnad90_sgproblem.pdf)
* [Bisk et al. - "Experience Grounds Language" (2020)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.10151)
* [Levesque et al. - "The Winograd Schema Challenge" (2012)](https://cdn.aaai.org/ocs/4492/4492-21843-1-PB.pdf)
* [Lake et al. - "Building Machines That Learn and Think Like People" (2017)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.00289)
